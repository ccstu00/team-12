We want to build a site for the UMass community where students can post and find items and services. 
Users must sign in with their UMass email and SPIRE ID to keep the site within the campus. 
This can help students save money, find things they need, and even connect with other students.
This project will affect campus students. The issue that our app addresses is the issue regarding advertising sellables and services on campus; there is no way to advertise anything on campus unless it's
via social media. Our app will change this by providing a space for UMass students to promote services, sellables, and community to everyone on campus, without the oversaturation of content outside of
campus. Community isn't exclusive to certain niches like events, clubs, and other school-related happenings, and we hope our project addresses the lack of general community on campus.
